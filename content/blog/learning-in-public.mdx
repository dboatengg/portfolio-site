Learning in public is a powerful accelerator. It invites feedback, connection, and accountability.

**1. Teaching forces understanding**

You don't fully understand something until you can explain it clearly. Writing about what you're learning tends to expose the gaps that tutorials quietly skip over.

**2. It builds a record, not just a post**

When you share solutions, reflections, or even failed attempts, you're not just posting, you're building a searchable trail of how you got better.

**3. The network effect**

Someone, somewhere, might find your post useful. That someone could be a future mentor, employer, or co-founder.

Every post plants a seed. Small on its own, but the more you write and share, the more they add up into something that actually represents the work you've put in.
